Executive Leadership

The Big Five — Executive Committee (EXCO)

Role: Strategic Leadership & Governance

The highest decision-making body within Branding Whizz (Pty) Ltd, composed of five senior executives responsible for strategic leadership, governance, and overall organizational performance.

The EXCO is mandated to guide the company using their expertise, experience, and professional judgment to ensure sustainable growth, operational excellence, and profitability. Each EXCO member oversees a specific department, ensuring that departmental strategies align with the organization's vision and contribute directly to revenue generation and long-term value creation.

Chief Executive Officer (CEO)

Strategic direction & overall success.

Chief Operations Officer (COO)

Day-to-day operational management & department execution.

HR Officer Permanent Secretary

Responsible for financial health, sustainability, and accountability.

Responsible for branding, market positioning, and revenue generation.

Chief Technology Officer (CTO)

Technological infrastructure and development of the EIS platform.

Organizational Structure

Our Specialized Departments

Working collaboratively to create visible, sustainable brand solutions.

1.1

Khudu

ICT Engineering & Digital

Role: Technology Development & Systems Management

The Khudu Department is responsible for the design, development, maintenance, and security of all technological systems, including the Educational Institutional Search Portal (EIS) platform and other projects of the organization. It ensures systems are efficient, scalable, and aligned with business needs.

Functions: Website Design & Revamp, SEO strategies, Google Business Optimization, and AI-powered web development to ensure a winning online presence.

1.2

Lengau

Marketing & Advertising

Role: Brand Growth & Market Positioning

This department leads marketing strategies, advertising campaigns, and brand positioning. It promotes Branding Whizz and its services, drives visibility, and supports revenue generation through strategic marketing initiatives.

Functions: Market surveys, promotional campaigns, and introducing the B2B Directory & Business Clinic.
1.3

Phuti

Printing, Production & Events

Role: Production & Event Execution

The Phuti Department handles all physical production, including printing, branding materials, and event management. It ensures high-quality delivery of promotional materials and successful execution of corporate and outreach events.

Functions: High-quality print, signage, embroidery, and magazine production collaborating with stakeholders.
1.4

Tshephe

Multimedia & Content Creation

Role: Creative Production & Content Development

This department develops all digital and visual content for Branding Whizz, including graphics, videos, and educational materials for the Educational Institutional Search Portal (EIS) Programme, B2B Directory, Designs etc. It ensures high-quality, engaging, and brand-aligned content across all platforms.

Functions: Digital storytelling, video production, animations, and high-impact content marketing strategy & planning.
1.5

Noko

Public Relations & Communications

Role: Corporate Image & Stakeholder Communication

The Noko Department manages the organization's public image, media relations, and internal/external communication. It ensures consistent messaging, builds brand reputation, and maintains relationships with stakeholders, partners, and the public.

Functions: Public image, press releases, media presence, and comprehensive social media campaign management.
1.6

Nkwe

Sales & Client Relations

Role: Revenue Generation & Client Management

The Nkwe Department is responsible for driving sales, securing clients, and maintaining strong client relationships. It ensures customer satisfaction, manages contracts, and converts opportunities into sustainable revenue streams for the organization.

Functions: Converting prospects, structuring sales packages, and ensuring consistent client relationships & follow-ups.
1.7

Matshwane

Research & Innovations

Role: Research and Innovation Developments

This department conducts research to improve existing services and develop new solutions. It drives innovation within the organization, ensuring that programmes like the Educational Institutional Search Portal (EIS) remain relevant, competitive, and impactful.

1.10

Thutlwa

Administration & Finance

Role: Organizational Support & Coordination

The Thutlwa Department provides administrative support across all departments. It manages documentation, coordination, logistics, and internal processes to ensure the organization operates efficiently and professionally.

Functions: Budgeting, accounting, compliance, reporting, and holistic financial sustainability.

Wosei Trust (Outreach Projects)

Role: Community Engagement & Social Impact

Empowering the women of Botswana through social impact.

Wosei Trust leads all outreach and community-based initiatives. It focuses on social impact programmes, educational engagement, and partnerships with institutions, ensuring the organization contributes meaningfully to societies of Botswana. It's an NGO generating revenue from Branding Whizz (Pty) Limited.

Wosei Trust remains the social impact originator and community engagement driver, while Branding Whizz serves as the implementation, technology, and commercialization vehicle for Wosei Trust.

The central hub for branding initiatives and the operational force supporting Wosei Trust.
