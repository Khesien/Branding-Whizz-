Educational Institutional Search Portal (EIS) Programme

Educational Institutional Search Portal

The Educational Institutional Search Portal (EIS) Programme aims to empower secondary students to make informed choices about their education. It focuses on enhancing accessibility to information, streamlining the search process, and providing personalized institution matches.

The Educational Institutional Search Portal (EIS) is a structured system that guides learners on subjects, courses & institutions — matching learners to regional & international universities. It supports exemptions, sponsorships & career planning. The portal app serves as the central tool to achieve these objectives, fostering educational inclusivity and support.
