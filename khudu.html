Technical Excellence & Standards

To maintain professional business operations, the Khudu Department adheres to strict performance KPIs:

  • Speed: All platforms are optimized to load in under 2 seconds.
  • Reliability: Zero tolerance for data breaches and a recovery time objective (RTO) of 4 hours in case of emergencies.
  • Automation: Internal forms feature auto-timestamps, unique reference numbers, and automated filing to streamline workflows.
